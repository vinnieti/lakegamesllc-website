Gameplay Highlights

BUILT FOR HIGH-STAKES DECISIONS

🎲

5-DICE RISK-REWARD

Roll five dice on every turn. Clear the 650-point entry gate, dodge the Full House penalty, and trigger Hot Dice to keep the streak alive.

🏦

BANKED TRAY

Stage scoring singles, sets, and straights in a recessed 3D tray—lock them in for points or return dice to reshape your strategy.

⚡

LIVE MULTIPLAYER

Challenge friends in real-time online rooms, pass the phone in local multiplayer, or sharpen your nerve against the computer.

♛

3D LUXURY TABLE

Crafted with obsidian dice, 24K gold pips, tactile animations, and crisp portrait and landscape layouts for phones and tablets.

The Studio

LAKE GAMES LLC

Lake Games LLC is an independent mobile game studio based in Belchertown, Massachusetts. We design focused, atmospheric tabletop and strategy experiences with tactile 3D craft and fair, transparent gameplay.

Our debut title, Last Roll, brings classic five-dice push-your-luck tension to iOS and Android with modern real-time multiplayer and zero clutter.
